Numana Digital

Junior SEO Specialist & GoHighLevel Assistant
Client type
Healthcare & service businesses
Duration
Mar 2021 – May 2026
Focus
SEO strategy + GoHighLevel automation
Overview

Numana Digital serves a portfolio of healthcare and service-based clients. Each one needed stronger organic visibility and the systems to handle the leads that visibility brings in.

My role covered both halves: SEO execution on one side, GoHighLevel build-out on the other.

SEO work

  • Led keyword research and competitive analysis to shape on-page SEO strategy across the client portfolio.
  • Ran Local SEO, including Google Business Profile optimisation and citation building, to lift local search rankings.

GoHighLevel automation

  • Handled A2P verification to keep SMS automations compliant and deliverable.
  • Built and maintained automations, funnels and websites inside GoHighLevel.
  • Set up appointment scheduling flows to cut manual back-and-forth.
  • Ran Google review campaigns to build local trust signals.
  • Connected social media posting to the automation layer.

Why both halves matter

Rankings bring in enquiries, but a slow reply loses them. That's why the SEO work was paired with automated SMS replies, booking flows and review requests in GoHighLevel.
