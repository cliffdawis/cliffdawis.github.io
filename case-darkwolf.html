Dark Wolf Collectibles

SEO Specialist & Social Media Manager
Client type
E-commerce collectibles brand
Duration
Jul 2023 – Jul 2025
Focus
On-page SEO, local listings & content
Overview

Dark Wolf Collectibles needed stronger search visibility and a consistent presence across the listing platforms that drive discovery for niche e-commerce.

I owned SEO and social media management for the account.

SEO & listings work

  • Ran a full SEO analysis and optimised on-page elements for better search visibility.
  • Set up and refined listings on Google Business Profile, Bing Places and Apple Maps, so the brand could be found beyond Google.
  • Wrote a blog content strategy aimed at growing monthly website traffic.

Social media management

I ran the brand's social media alongside the SEO work, so content strategy and organic search worked toward the same traffic goals instead of as two separate channels.
